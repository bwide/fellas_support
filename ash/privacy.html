Privacy Policy for Ash

Last updated 28 October 2026

Ash is a fiction app: you talk to invented characters. This page explains what the app does with your data. In short — there is no account, your conversations stay on your phone, and the only thing that leaves your device is the text the AI needs to answer you.

What stays on your device

  • Your conversations with the characters, including the name you choose. They are stored in the app's local database and are not uploaded to us.
  • Your settings — the pace and voice you picked, your selected cast, and which conversations you have open.
  • Deleting a conversation in the app deletes it. Deleting the app deletes everything above.

Ash does not use iCloud sync, so this data is not backed up to any server we control.

What is sent off your device

  • Messages, to the AI provider. When you send a message, the recent part of that conversation is sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) together with the character's instructions, so it can write the reply. If you ask the app to draw a scene, the text you asked for is sent the same way. We do not use your messages to train models, and our AI provider processes them under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Purchase state, to Apple and RevenueCat. If you buy a subscription, Apple processes the payment. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and the purchase receipt so the app can tell whether the subscription is active. We never see your card or your Apple Account.
  • Usage events, to our analytics provider. The app records anonymous events — screens opened, a purchase started, an error — tied to a random identifier generated on your device. These events contain no message text and no name.

What we do not do

  • No account, no email address, no phone number, no password.
  • No advertising SDK, and no selling or renting of data to anyone.
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites, so the app never asks for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • No access to your location, contacts, calendar, photos or microphone, unless you choose to save a generated image to your photo library.

Retention

Conversations live on your iPhone until you delete them. Analytics events are kept by our provider for a limited period and then expire. Purchase records are held by Apple and RevenueCat for as long as accounting and tax rules require.

Age

Ash is intended for adults aged 17 and over. It contains romantic and emotional themes but no explicit sexual content. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 17.

Your choices

  • Delete any conversation from inside the app; delete the app to remove everything local.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.
  • Write to us to ask what analytics we hold for your device identifier, or to ask us to erase it.

Changes

When this policy changes we update the date at the top and, for a material change, say so in the app's release notes.
